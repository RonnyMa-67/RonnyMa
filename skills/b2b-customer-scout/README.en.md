# 🌍 B2B Customer Scout

**Find companies that fit your product**

Use product specifications, images, and target markets to find companies, verify their fit, identify public business contacts, and check visit locations.

[↓ Download ZIP](https://github.com/RonnyMa-67/RonnyMa/releases/download/skills-2026-11-06/b2b-customer-scout.zip) · [SKILL.md](SKILL.md)

## 🧭 Give it a starting point

Provide your product, specifications or reference images, target market, customer types, and requested count. Ask for matching companies, public contacts, and supporting evidence.

## 📎 Before you start

Install the downloaded folder in a Skills-compatible AI host. This package does not provide its own AI service or bypass the host’s limits. Current research needs browsing. Evidence still needs review; a company match does not prove buying intent.
