# Public-source research standard

Research each client from its exact company name, location, email domain, official website, business directories and public LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram. Search local-language spelling and transliterations when appropriate.

Grade every claim: verified (primary source), supported (independent matching signals), candidate (unconfirmed namesake), or unknown. A same-name result, self-reported job title or generic company name is never proof of affiliation. Preserve exact sources and claims.

Separate personal lead contact details from published company contact points. Check official website contact/about pages and cross-links to company socials. A Facebook profile does not guarantee a working Messenger endpoint. Normalize international WA links only from explicitly provided WhatsApp numbers; do not conflate a generic phone number with verified WhatsApp.

Use public information and available free tools. Do not assume paid registries, authenticated web access, or API quotas. Do not publish lead information.
