# Business writing and ratings

## Company summary

Use separate lines within one cell:

```
North Quay Café Group｜餐饮及咖啡门店
主营：经营咖啡、果汁及简餐，面向当地消费群体。
背景：社区门店，公开菜单包含冷饮。
规模：企业公开资料列示3家门店。
```

These are fictional details. Omit the scale line when unknown; never fabricate numbers. If no reliable corporate match exists, describe business direction as self-reported rather than invent a registered company. Keep sourcing and doubts in a field note, not an oversized main cell.

## Rank

- P1: substantiated strong operational fit, distribution/channel opportunity or concrete procurement project.
- P2: relevant usage or customer interest, worth a follow-up question.
- P3: weaker current fit but a credible exploratory opportunity remains.
- 待核实: insufficient core corporate identity or essential evidence.

Company size alone does not determine priority. Event interest does not mean a confirmed meeting. Do not conflate sales responsiveness with lead quality.

## Voice

Write short, natural operational Chinese. Describe what the company does, why it might need the product, and one useful next question. Avoid buzzwords, repetitive disclaimers, generic “send catalogue and confirm voltage” suggestions, and marketing exaggeration.
