# Writing and commercial prioritization

## Recommended style

Use confident, natural, positive **sales intelligence** writing: describe what a person/organization does, how its business connects with the requested equipment, and what the representative should do next. Avoid repeatedly prefacing every sentence with `可能、推测、尚未核实`, self-protective boilerplate and templated promises. Build credible hypotheses while keeping material uncertainty in citations/notes or one tight qualifier.

For each lead:

**D company introduction**: identifiable person/company / 主营 / 背景 / verified 规模 or relevant 渠道 direction. Richer than an identity checklist, not an invented company profile. If no reliable independent enterprise record exists, describe form business type as the client's stated operating context and combine it with sourced adjacent context. Do not label a self-reported name as a registered entity without proof.

**H match & opportunity**: 1) how the chosen product serves the business use case, 2) procurement scope/timing or market development opportunity, 3) specific collaboration route such as sample testing, channel introduction or solution bundling, without declaring confirmed orders.

**J next step**: one concise, businesslike action tied to what the lead *actually* selected (e.g. request beverage category and footprint for selection; share slush machine specifications for a 1–5 unit plan; arrange booth check-in only where the client selected on-site interest). Do not invent VIP perks, discount or exclusivity.

## Priority

- P1: concrete product + quantity/timeframe or credible high-value channel with next action.
- P2: relevant product or business direction with workable sales conversation.
- P3: weak current match but identifiable prospecting direction.
- 待核实: missing enough primary intent to recommend a concrete next step; reserve, do not overuse.

Do not assign priority from blank company size, the telephone prefix, or a generic “interest=deal”. Positive tone does not change evidence status.

## Anti-patterns

- Copying form machine codes into D/H/J (`multiple_categories_/_need_selection_advice`). Translate to “多品类设备，需要选型建议” and preserve raw code in input/comment.
- Turning `3 months` into “每月采购” or turning “meet at trade show” into “confirmed booked VIP visit”.
- Assuming a business is a café franchise because a form choice says restaurant, or identifying national market from WA code.
- Writing 5 identical essays for 5 different buyers or giving every lead the same generic catalogue/discount script.
- Putting all contact channels in the last seven extra columns in a fixed-layout master.
