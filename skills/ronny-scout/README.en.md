# 🔎 Ronny Scout

**Make sense of the leads you already have**

Keep the original request, research the company and public contacts, then organize commercial fit, priorities, and the next step.

An inquiry should lead somewhere. This one keeps the original demand intact, separates evidence from guesses, and turns research into a fixed ten-column sheet. Fewer generic “follow up further” notes, hopefully 🫠

[↓ Download ZIP](https://github.com/RonnyMa-67/RonnyMa/releases/download/skills-2026-11-06/ronny-scout.zip) · [SKILL.md](SKILL.md)

[🎮 Interactive demo](https://ronnyma-67.github.io/RonnyMa/projects/ronny-scout/)

## 🧭 Give it a starting point

Attach your lead records and ask: “Use Ronny Scout to research these leads, preserve the original requests, verify public sources, and produce the A–J research sheet.”

## 📎 Before you start

Install the downloaded folder in a Skills-compatible AI host. This package does not provide its own AI service or bypass the host’s limits. Current research needs browsing. Evidence still needs review; a company match does not prove buying intent.

## 🛠️ Optional spreadsheet tools

The original installation notes, MIT license, A–J schema, local commands and test instructions are preserved in [the technical guide](GUIDE.en.md). The scripts format researched data; they do not perform web research.
