# 私信自动化五件套模板（英文 · 占位符版）

用于 Meta Business Suite → 收件箱（Inbox）→ 自动化（Automations）里粘贴启用。面向海外客户，全英文。占位符：

- `{{COMPANY}}` 品牌名
- `{{PRODUCT_LINES}}` 产品线（如 juice dispensers, slush machines, instant beverage machines, wine dispensers）
- `{{BUSINESS_HOURS}}` 工作时间（含时区，如 Mon–Fri, 9:00–17:30 Beijing time, UTC+8）
- `{{BOOTH}}` 展位号（仅展会期快捷回复用）

**红线：以下所有自动文案只接住人和收集信息，绝不自动承诺价格、MOQ、交期、运费；一律引导到人工报价。** 启用前经所有者逐条确认。

---

## 1. Instant Reply（首次私信自动回复）

> Hi, thanks for contacting {{COMPANY}}! We supply commercial beverage equipment — {{PRODUCT_LINES}} and more.
>
> To help you faster, please tell us:
> 1) Your business type (cafe, restaurant, hotel, distributor, etc.)
> 2) The drinks you want to serve
> 3) Your country and city
>
> Our team will reply during business hours ({{BUSINESS_HOURS}}).

## 2. Away Message（非工作时间自动回复）

> Hi, thanks for your message! We're currently outside our business hours ({{BUSINESS_HOURS}}) and will reply on the next business day.
>
> Please leave the product you're interested in and your WhatsApp number if you'd like a faster follow-up.

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions（聊天窗口可点选的问题）

**Q1: What products do you offer?**
A: We offer commercial beverage equipment including {{PRODUCT_LINES}} and smart beverage solutions. Tell us your business type and the drinks you plan to serve, and we'll recommend suitable models.

**Q2: How can I get a price?**
A: Prices depend on the model, quantity and destination. Please share the product you're interested in, the quantity you need, and your country/city — our team will send you a quotation.

**Q3: Do you support OEM / ODM?**
A: Yes, we can discuss OEM/ODM options. Please tell us the product, estimated quantity and your branding requirements, and our team will follow up with details.

**Q4: Can you send me your catalogue?**
A: Of course. Please tell us which product category you're interested in and your email or WhatsApp number, and we'll send the catalogue and specifications.

**Q5: Do you ship to my country?**
A: Please share your country and destination city/port, and the product and quantity you need — our team will check the shipping options for you.

## 4. Keyword Replies（关键词触发）

**Keywords: price / quote / cost**
> Thanks for asking. To quote accurately, please share the model or product type, quantity, and your country/city. Our team will send a quotation during business hours.

**Keywords: catalogue / catalog / brochure**
> Please tell us which product category you need and your email or WhatsApp number — we'll send the catalogue and specs.

**Keywords: OEM / ODM**
> We can discuss OEM/ODM for our beverage equipment. Please share the product, estimated quantity and branding requirements, and our team will follow up.

## 5. Saved Replies（人工快捷回复，业务员手动发）

**Ask for WhatsApp:**
> Thanks for your interest! Could you share your WhatsApp number? It will be easier for us to send you photos, videos, specifications and a quotation.

**Follow-up qualifying:**
> To recommend the right model, may I ask: what type of business is it for, how many drinks do you expect to serve per day, and which drinks are on your menu?

**Exhibition follow-up（展会期用，展位号占位）:**
> Great to connect after the show! We are at Booth {{BOOTH}}. If you'd like model details, specifications or a quotation, tell us the product you checked at the booth and your WhatsApp number, and we'll follow up right away.
