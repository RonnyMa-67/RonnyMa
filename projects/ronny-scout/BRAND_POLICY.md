# Ronny Scout — Brand & attribution guidance

Ronny Scout is an independently maintained open-source lead intelligence project.

## Code rights and existing releases

- The code previously published under MIT remains governed by that license. The MIT license permits commercial use, copying and redistribution **provided the required copyright and permission notices are preserved**.
- This guidance does **not** revoke rights granted by existing MIT versions and does not override any applicable source-code license.
- Future license changes must be explicitly announced with version information; they cannot retrospectively remove permissions already granted.

## Identity and attribution

- Respect the copyright notice and exact license obligations when reusing code.
- Do not falsely claim to be the original creator, the official Ronny Scout product, or endorsed by its maintainer.
- Use your own name, logo and presentation if you redistribute a fork as an independent product. Avoid confusingly similar branding.
- Distinguish third-party commercial services from official updates. Users should never be misled about a product's source or affiliation.

The Ronny Scout name and visual brand identity are used to identify the maintainer's own project. This document is a project naming and attribution policy, not a claim of a registered trademark or a substitute for legal advice.

## Respectful commercial collaboration

If you are building commercial support, training, hosting or integrations around Ronny Scout, be clear about attribution and independent status. Contact the maintainer to discuss an official collaboration or branded offering.

## Public demonstrations

Showcase sample companies, contacts and buying needs are fictional. Do not redistribute real people or organizations' confidential lead data in example datasets or documentation.
