I shaped business requirements, fields, and assessment rules, and used AI to help implement and iterate. Public customers are fictional or substituted. Demo scores do not prove real sales or purchasing capacity.

Each project states its current capabilities and setup. Real APIs, cloud services, WeChat permissions, and production deployments require your own environment. Third-party licenses remain in place; there is no blanket license for the entire repository.
